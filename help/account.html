Account & Profile Management Guide

Everything you need to know about creating an account, signing in with Google or Email/User ID, managing profile data, and resetting passwords.

1. How to Sign In or Create an Account

GoDoz Technology provides three flexible authentication methods powered by enterprise Firebase Authentication:

A One-Click Google Sign-In (Recommended)

Click "Continue with Google" on login.html. Your Google avatar, verified email, and full name will be instantly linked with zero manual forms required.

B Email or User ID & Password

You can enter either your full work email address or your customized User ID. Enter your password and click "Sign In to Account".

C Continue as Guest (Anonymous Access)

If you are exploring services or calculating project costs, click "Continue as Guest / Anonymous". You will be assigned a temporary session UID without giving your email.

2. Managing & Editing Your Profile

Once signed in, visit the Profile Dashboard (profile.html). Click on the "Edit Profile" tab to update:

  • Full Name / Display Name: Used for official software licenses and invoices.
  • WhatsApp & Phone Number: Enables priority direct communication for project updates.
  • Company & Business Name: Appears on customized invoices and Play Store app ownership documents.
  • Profile Avatar Photo: Custom URL or Google profile photo.
  • Billing Address: Used to generate GST compliant invoices for app orders.

3. Forgot Password & Account Recovery

If you forget your password, click the "Forgot?" link on the login page or use the "Send Password Reset Link" button inside your profile settings. Firebase will immediately dispatch a secure password reset link to your registered email address.
