Online Payment & Razorpay Guide

Learn how to pay securely for custom Android apps, websites, and milestone billing via Razorpay, UPI, cards, and net banking.

1. How to Make an Online Payment

GoDoz Technology uses Razorpay, India's most secure and trusted payment gateway. You can pay directly from your client dashboard:

1 Sign In to Your Portal

Log in at login.html with your Google account, Email/User ID, or Guest account.

2 Go to "Pay Online" Tab in Profile

Navigate to profile.html and click on the "Pay Online (Razorpay)" tab.

3 Select Milestone Amount & Pay

Choose a preset milestone (₹1,000 Booking Token, ₹2,500 Sprint Milestone, ₹5,000 Delivery Payment) or enter your custom agreed amount. Click "Pay Securely with Razorpay" to complete the transaction.

2. Supported Payment Methods

Our Razorpay checkout supports 100+ payment methods across India and international clients:

Google Pay PhonePe Paytm UPI Any UPI App / QR Visa / Mastercard / RuPay 50+ Net Banking Banks EMI / Pay Later

3. Pay After Demo (PAD) & 6 Progress Stages

To ensure 100% transparency and zero risk, custom projects follow our Pay After Demo (PAD) framework:

  • Stage 01 (5%): Order Registered with ₹0 Upfront Fees.
  • Stage 02 (15%): Requirements & Wireframe Demo Finalized on WhatsApp/Call.
  • Stage 03 (35%): Sample Demo Approved ➔ 10% Token Deposit Paid ➔ Active Coding Commences.
  • Stage 04 (65%): Core Development & Mid-Point Beta APK / Web Prototype.
  • Stage 05 (85%): Quality Assurance Testing & Client Beta Verification.
  • Stage 06 (100%): Final Milestone Settlement ➔ Live Launch & 100% Full Source Code Handover.

4. Cancellation & Refund Rules

Clear, explicit rules regarding order cancellation and refunds:

  • Before Demo Approval (Stage 1 & 2): 100% Free Cancellation with ₹0 fee if cancelled before approving the sample demo.
  • After Token Deposit (Stage 3+): The 10% token deposit covers initial domain/API registrations and engineering time expended once active coding starts.
  • Completed Milestones: Payments made for completed and approved milestones are non-refundable as engineering hours and source code assets have been delivered.

5. Late Payments, Testing Delays & "On Hold" Status

Clients receive 14 days to test beta APK builds or web prototypes at each milestone. If feedback or payments are delayed beyond 14 days without notice:

  • The order status is updated to "On Hold".
  • All previously paid records, source files, and database schemas are preserved safely in Firebase/GitHub.
  • The project can be reopened at any time via WhatsApp/Admin without penalty upon feedback or milestone settlement.

4. Invoices & Payment Receipts

Upon successful payment completion, an instant digital receipt is generated with Payment ID and Order Reference. A formal invoice detailing software development services is dispatched to your registered email (godoz.info@gmail.com).
