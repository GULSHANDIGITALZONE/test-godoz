1. Gemini AI & Smart Software Automation
🤖 How does GoDoz integrate Google Gemini AI into custom Android apps and Web Portals?

We integrate Google Gemini 1.5 Flash / Pro AI models via REST APIs and Firebase Vertex AI SDKs into custom Android apps and web dashboards. This enables automated customer support chatbots, smart invoice scanners, automated lead qualifiers, and PDF document OCR summarization directly inside your software.

⚡ What kind of automation tools can GoDoz build for small businesses in India?

We build custom automated messaging bots, GST invoice generators, POS receipt printers, automated WhatsApp campaign dispatchers, student attendance SMS engines, and land record / government portal search engines tailored to local Indian business workflows.

2. Google SEO, Speed & Core Web Vitals Optimization
📈 How does GoDoz ensure my website ranks high on Google Search?

Every website engineered by GoDoz includes 100/100 Core Web Vitals speed optimization, semantic HTML5 structure, OpenGraph social meta tags, canonical tags, automated XML sitemaps, robots.txt, and Schema.org (JSON-LD) structured data markup for instant Google Search Console indexing and high local SEO ranking.

📱 Will my custom website and Android app be 100% mobile responsive?

Yes! Every layout is tested on all screen sizes (phones, tablets, laptops, desktops) using CSS Flexbox, Grid, and Media Queries for seamless responsive viewing and fast loading speeds.

3. Android App Development & Play Store Publishing
🔑 Do I get full 100% source code ownership for my Android App and Website?

Yes! Upon milestone completion, you receive 100% full source code access on GitHub, Android Studio project files, build scripts, signing keystores, and complete intellectual property ownership with zero vendor lock-in or recurring hostage fees.

🚀 Does GoDoz handle Google Play Store publishing and Console certification?

Yes! We guide you through Google Play Console account setup, generate signed AAB App Bundles, create store listing graphics & privacy policy documents, and guide your app through Play Store policy review for a successful live launch.
