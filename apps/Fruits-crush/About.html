📖 How to Play

🍎

Basic Matching

Step 1 Swipe and match 3 or more identical fruits in a row or column.

Step 2 Match 4 fruits to create a Rocket Booster and 5 fruits to create a Bomb Booster!

🛠️ Using Power-Ups

🔨

Magic Hammer

Stuck on a tricky tile? Tap the Hammer button and then tap the tile to break it instantly without wasting moves.

✋

Magic Hand (Swap)

Want to swap any two adjacent fruits without using a move? Tap the Hand button, then tap the two fruits to swap them.

🚀

Super Rocket

Clear an entire row or column instantly by tapping the Rocket button and placing it on the target row.

🎡

Lucky Daily Spin

Running low on coins or boosters? Spin the Lucky Wheel on the home screen every day for free coins and boosters!

💡 Pro Tips & Strategies

  • Create Combos: Chain multiple matches together for "Sweet!" and "Amazing!" bonus multiplier scores.
  • Save Coins: Save coins for challenging boss levels so you can unlock extra hammers and moves.
  • Watch Rewards: Watch rewarded videos to earn 5 extra bonus moves when you run out of moves.
