About Next Gen Edu (NGE)

Next Gen Edu (NGE) (developed by RS GULSHAN PRAJAPATI) is an initiative under GoDoz Technology designed to empower students, competitive exam aspirants, and lifelong learners with organized digital learning tools.

The portal provides simplified access to state and national exam notifications, syllabus breakdowns, study notes, question banks, and direct portal links for competitive examinations (SSC, Railway, Banking, State PCS, and Police examinations).

Structured Study Notes
