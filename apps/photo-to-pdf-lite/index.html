About Photo to PDF Lite

Photo to PDF Lite (developed by RS GULSHAN PRAJAPATI) is a compact, high-efficiency scanner tool designed for users who want to digitize paper documents without heavy, battery-draining scanning apps.

Featuring intelligent on-device image processing (Google ML Kit), the app automatically crops borders, straightens angled photographs, enhances black-and-white contrast, and exports high-clarity PDF files in under 3 seconds. The entire process runs 100% offline without sending your personal paperwork to external servers.

Key Features & Capabilities

📐

Automatic Edge Detection

Smart machine vision identifies document boundaries and corrects perspective distortion automatically.

🎚️

Document Contrast Filters

Magic Color, Grayscale, and B&W enhancement filters remove shadows and sharpen text.

📴

100% Offline Processing

No cloud uploads required. Your sensitive paperwork stays safe and private on your phone.

📦

Batch Scanning & Export

Capture 20+ pages sequentially and combine them into a single organized, compressed PDF file.
