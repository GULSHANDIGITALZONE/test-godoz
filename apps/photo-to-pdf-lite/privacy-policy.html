Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 04, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Application.

1. Data Collection

Personal Data: We do not collect any personal identification information (such as name, email, or phone number).

Images & Files: All image processing and PDF generation are performed locally on your device. We do not upload your photos or documents to any servers.

2. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core app functionality.
  • AdMob (Google Mobile Ads): Used to display advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers to show personalized ads.
  • Google ML Kit: Used for document scanning. Image processing for scanning happens entirely on-device.

3. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions to function correctly:

  • Camera: To scan documents and take photos.
  • Storage/Media: To select images from your gallery and save the generated PDFs.
  • Internet: To load advertisements and initialize analytics.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
