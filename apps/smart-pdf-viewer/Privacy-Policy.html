2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or the content of your PDF files to our servers. All document processing (reading, merging, compressing, etc.) happens locally on your mobile device.

Permissions Required for Core Functionality:

MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (All Files Access)

To provide advanced PDF editing tools such as Merging multiple files, Compressing files to save space, Renaming, and Deleting files outside the app's private folder, this permission is strictly necessary. It also enables the "Duplicate File Finder" and "Large File Finder" features.

CAMERA

Required for the "Scan to PDF" feature to capture images of physical documents for conversion.
