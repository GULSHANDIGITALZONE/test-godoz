About PDF Master

PDF Master is your complete PDF solution. It is built to be fast, offline, and very easy to use. No more worrying about heavy files or complicated editors.

How to Use PDF Master

1. View PDF Files

Open the app, all your PDFs will be listed automatically. Just tap to read. Use "Night Mode" for comfortable reading in low light.

2. Merge PDF Documents

Go to Tools > Merge PDF. Select two or more files and click "Merge". Your combined file will be saved in Downloads.

3. Compress PDF Size

Select any PDF, choose the quality level (Low / Medium / High), and click Compress. It reduces file size while maintaining clarity.

4. Image to PDF Converter

Select photos from your gallery, crop or rotate them if needed, and click "Create PDF". Perfect for official documents!
