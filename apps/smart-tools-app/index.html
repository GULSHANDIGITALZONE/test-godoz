About QR Scanner & Setting Hub

QR Scanner & Setting Hub (developed by RS GULSHAN PRAJAPATI) is an indispensable Android utility engineered to make everyday device tasks fast, simple, and bloatware-free.

Instead of installing 5 separate apps for scanning QR codes, reading barcodes, creating Wi-Fi share codes, toggling flashlight modes, and digging through nested Android settings menus, QR Scanner & Setting Hub unites all these essential tools into a single lightweight application. It features automated autofocus scanning, scan history logging, copy-to-clipboard actions, and direct deep-link shortcuts to hidden Android settings.

Official Privacy Policy for QR Scanner & Setting Hub

Last Updated: August 2026

GoDoz Technology and founder RS GULSHAN PRAJAPATI respect your privacy. QR Scanner & Setting Hub is designed with privacy-first principles where all scanning and tool processing occur locally on your device:

1. Zero Personal Data Harvesting

The app does not collect, sell, or transmit personal data (such as your name, contacts, phone number, or scanned QR contents) to remote servers. All scanned barcode and QR payloads are processed in real-time on your device's memory.

2. Permissions Requested & Justifications

  • Camera: Required strictly to scan QR codes and barcodes through the camera lens.
  • Flashlight / Torch: Used to illuminate QR codes in low-light environments and operate the torch utility.
  • Storage (Save QR Image): Used strictly when the user chooses to export a generated QR code image to their gallery.
  • Internet & Network State: Used solely to load advertisements (Google AdMob) and verify web link connectivity when opening scanned URLs.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Play Services and Google AdMob. AdMob may utilize anonymous advertising identifiers according to Google's standard advertising policies.
