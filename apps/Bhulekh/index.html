About Bhulekh Bihar Land Records Utility

Bhulekh Bihar Utility (developed by RS GULSHAN PRAJAPATI) is an informational digital service directory providing citizens with organized, instant navigation to official Bihar Land Revenue (Biharbhumi) services.

Citizens often find official government portals difficult to navigate on mobile browsers. This utility aggregates critical direct links for Jamabandi Panji verification, Khasra/Khesra plot details, Khatiyan maps, Dakhil Kharij (mutation) application tracking, LPC status, and online land tax (Lagaan) payment in a clean, high-speed mobile interface.

Key Portals & Verification Services

📜

Jamabandi & Khatiyan Check

Look up owner names, plot numbers, Khata numbers, and land boundaries across all Bihar districts.

🔍

Dakhil Kharij (Mutation) Status

Track your mutation case number, rejection reasons, and circle officer approvals in real time.

💳

Online Lagaan & Land Tax

Check pending dues and make online payments for agricultural and residential land receipts.

📑

LPC Application & Download

Apply for Land Possession Certificate (LPC) and download verified official certificates.
