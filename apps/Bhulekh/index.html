About Mera Bhulekh (com.gdz.bhulekh)

Mera Bhulekh (developed by RS GULSHAN PRAJAPATI under GoDoz Technology) is a high-speed mobile application and citizen utility portal created to simplify navigation for citizens searching for Bihar Land Revenue records (Biharbhumi).

Searching government portals on mobile browsers can often be cumbersome. Mera Bhulekh brings together official public portal links for Jamabandi Panji verification, Khasra/Khesra plot details, Khatiyan maps, Dakhil Kharij (mutation) application status, Land Possession Certificate (LPC) tracking, and online land tax (Lagaan) payment into a clean, responsive Android experience.

How to Check Bihar Land Record Online (Jamabandi Kaise Dekhein)

  1. Open Mera Bhulekh App or click on the Open Bihar Bhumi Portal button above.
  2. Select District & Circle: Choose your District (Zila) and Circle (Anchal) from the dropdown list.
  3. Select Mauza / Village: Choose your Mauza name or enter the Halka details.
  4. Search Record: Enter Khata Number, Khasra/Plot Number, Jamabandi Number, or Raiyat (Owner) Name.
  5. View & Save: Click on Search to view your official Jamabandi document and save or print for reference.
