2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

When you use the Application, specifically the "Sign in with Google" feature, we collect limited personal information provided by Google, which includes:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture (URL only)

B. User-Generated Data

If you use the "Save Record" feature within the Application, we store the specific record details (such as Khasra number, District, or Village name) in our secure cloud database (Firebase). This data is linked to your account to allow you to access your saved records across multiple devices.

C. Device Information

We may collect non-personal information about your mobile device, including the device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for the purpose of troubleshooting and improving app performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the services of the Application.
  • To manage your user account and provide personalized features.
  • To synchronize your "Saved Records" across your devices via our cloud infrastructure.
  • To respond to your support requests or inquiries.
  • To monitor app usage and detect, prevent, or address technical issues.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your information is only shared in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use Google Firebase for authentication and database management. These services are bound by their own security and privacy standards.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can do so through the settings menu in the Application or by contacting us at our support email.
