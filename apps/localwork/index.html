About LocalWork (काम करो)

LocalWork (काम करो) is a mission-driven digital employment platform created by RS GULSHAN PRAJAPATI (GoDoz Technology). In traditional job hiring, blue-collar workers and daily-wage laborers lose a significant portion of their income to contractors and intermediary agents. LocalWork bridges this gap by directly connecting nearby job providers (homeowners, builders, shops, small businesses) with registered local skilled workers.

Whether you need an electrician, plumber, mason, carpenter, house painter, driver, cook, farm worker, or general helper, LocalWork provides real-time location-based searching, direct phone calling, and in-app instant chat with 100% transparent pricing and zero commission deductions.

Official Privacy Policy for LocalWork

Last Updated: August 2026

GoDoz Technology and founder RS GULSHAN PRAJAPATI built LocalWork as a free-to-use public employment platform. This PRIVACY POLICY explains how user data is collected, used, and protected:

1. Information Collection & Use

To facilitate job listings and worker discoverability, the app collects profile information provided voluntarily: Worker Name, Mobile Number, Trade/Skill Category, Experience, and approximate Location/Address. This data is displayed strictly on public worker listings to allow potential clients to hire you.

2. Permissions Requested

  • Location (Coarse/Fine): To sort and recommend nearby jobs and workers within your geographic area.
  • Phone Call / Dialer Access: To initiate direct calls between employers and workers upon user action.
  • Camera & Storage: To upload worker profile photos or job site photos.
  • Internet: To sync job listings, profiles, and live updates with the server.

3. Data Security & Third-Party Services

All communication is protected by HTTPS encryption. We do not sell or rent personal information to external marketers. The app integrates Google Play Services and Google Firebase for authentication and database synchronization.

4. Account & Data Deletion

Users can edit or delete their profile and listed job posts anytime within the app or by submitting a deletion request to godoz.info@gmail.com.

5. Contacting the Developer
