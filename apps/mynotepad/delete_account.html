How to Delete Your Cloud Account

For your security, account deletion can only be initiated from within the MyNotepad Android App.

Steps to Delete Your Cloud Data:

1

Open the MyNotepad app on your Android device.

2

Tap on Cloud Account.

3

Go to Settings (gear icon) from the side menu.

4

Select Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.

5

Wait for the 30-second security timer to finish and confirm the deletion.

Warning: Deleting your account will permanently remove all your Cloud Notes and Tasks from our servers.

Note: Your personal Google Drive backups (Photos, Videos, etc.) are NOT affected and will remain in your Google account.
